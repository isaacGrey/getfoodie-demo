About this demonstration

GetFoodie is a practice and portfolio showcase. This demo is a standalone, browser-based reconstruction of the app's key customer, restaurant owner, and admin flows.

No backend or real accounts are connected. Ordering, payment options, statuses and approvals are simulated and saved locally in your browser. This is not a live food-delivery service.
